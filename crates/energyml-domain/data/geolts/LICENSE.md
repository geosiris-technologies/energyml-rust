# BRGM GeolTs register (chronostratigraphic chart)

`GeolTs.csv` and `GeolTsBound.csv` are exports of the `GeolTs` and `GeolTsBound`
registers of the BRGM (Bureau de Recherches Geologiques et Minieres), fetched
on 24/09/2026 from <https://data.geoscience.fr/ncl/GeolTs>.
